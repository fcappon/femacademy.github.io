Cookie Policy

Last updated: 23 March 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them on the FemAcademy website, and how you can control them.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They help the website remember information about your visit, making it easier to use and more useful to you.

Cookies do not contain viruses or malware. They cannot access other files on your device.

How we use cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

1. Analytics cookies (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. This helps us improve the user experience.

Cookie name Purpose Duration
_ga Distinguishes unique visitors 2 years
_gid Distinguishes unique visitors 24 hours
_gat Throttles request rate 1 minute

Google Analytics cookies collect information in an anonymous form, including the number of visitors to the site, where visitors came from, and the pages they visited.

What we don't use

We do not use:

  • Advertising cookies
  • Marketing cookies
  • Social media tracking cookies
  • Third-party cookies (except Google Analytics)

Local storage (not a cookie)

We use browser localStorage to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode). This is not a cookie — it's stored locally in your browser and is never sent to our servers or any third party.

You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's local storage.

How to control or delete cookies

Browser settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • Block all cookies
  • Block third-party cookies only
  • Delete cookies after each browsing session
  • Accept cookies only from specific websites

However, blocking all cookies may affect your experience on this and other websites. Some features may not work properly.

Opt out of Google Analytics

To opt out of Google Analytics across all websites, you can install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Browser-specific instructions

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge
  • Opera

More information

For more information about cookies and how they work, visit www.allaboutcookies.org.
