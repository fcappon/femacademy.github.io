About your tutor

PhD-qualified, AFHEA-certified, and over 10 years of tutoring experience.

Hi, I'm Femke. I hold a PhD in Biomedical Engineering and an Associate Fellowship of the Higher Education Academy (AFHEA), and I have been tutoring Maths and Physics for more than 10 years — from GCSE foundations to A-Level exam preparation and university-level problem sets.

Alongside tutoring, I design technical training at a UK national research institute, where my day job is making genuinely difficult scientific content clear and accessible. That is exactly what I bring to lessons: I explain the why behind each method, break problems into steps, and build the confidence to tackle exam questions independently.

I am DBS checked, 5-star rated on Tutorful, and I teach students across the UK online.
